Legal

Privacy policy

The full policy is being finalised with counsel and will be published here. A company that sells GDPR and DPDP compliance should not ship a template, so this page states plainly what applies today rather than saying nothing.

What this site collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising pixel and no tracking cookie on aphelioncyber.com. Every interactive instrument on the site runs entirely in your browser, and the contact intake composes a message in your own mail client rather than posting anywhere.

What we hold when you contact us

If you email or call us, we hold what you send — your name, organisation, contact details and whatever you tell us — for as long as it takes to answer you and, where you become a client, for the duration of the engagement and any retention period the contract or the law requires.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who it has been shared with, ask us to correct or erase it, nominate someone to exercise those rights on your behalf, and raise a grievance. Write to secure@aphelioncyber.com and we will respond.
