The capability constellation

Thirty-six cybersecurity services, and how they hold together.

Every cybersecurity service Aphelion Cyber delivers in India and the UAE, arranged the way security actually works rather than the way a price list is organised: what you build to stop an attack, what you run to find one, and what you have standing by for the day one lands.

Most engagements begin in one group and pull in the next. A penetration test finds the identity path; identity and access management closes it; the managed SOC notices the next attempt. Nothing on this map stands entirely alone, and the connections below are the ones we see most.
