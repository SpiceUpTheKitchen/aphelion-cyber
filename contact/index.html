What happens next

03 / 03

Four steps, and no salesperson at any of them.

  1. 01

    You get a reply from an engineer

    Within one business day, from somebody who can answer a technical question rather than route it. In an active incident, immediately, by phone.

  2. 02

    Forty-five minutes on your environment

    Your architecture, your obligations, your deadline. If a different provider or a cheaper service fits better, we will say so on this call rather than after the invoice.

  3. 03

    A free readiness assessment

    Against the framework you are actually being asked for — ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA or the DPDP Act. It is a gap list with owners, not a brochure.

  4. 04

    A written scope, then work

    Fixed scope, named people, dates. Nothing starts before you have the rules of engagement in writing, and nothing production-facing starts without a named contact who can stop it.
