Aphelion — the point in an orbit farthest from the sun

About Aphelion Cyber: founded 2024, and we say so.

Aphelion Cyber is a cybersecurity consultancy and platform company working from Ahmedabad and Sharjah. We help organisations build a proactive and resilient security posture through risk management and governance, security architecture, identity and access management, cloud and network security, and incident response.

The company was incorporated in 2024. That is a young company, and there is no version of this page that pretends otherwise — you will not find “over a decade of experience” anywhere on this site, because the company has not existed for a decade. The experience belongs to the people, it is considerable, and it is named below with the years attached to the person who earned them.

In a category that sells almost entirely through fear, we would rather show you the room. Every interactive instrument on this site runs on labelled sample data and teaches one thing you can go and verify yourself. That is the whole marketing strategy.

What we are for

03 / 05

Mission, vision, and three values that cost something.

Mission

To help businesses build a proactive and resilient cybersecurity posture, through consulting in risk management, governance, security architecture, identity and access management, cloud and network security, and incident response.

Vision

To become a trusted global leader in cybersecurity consulting, empowering organisations to navigate the evolving digital landscape through innovative, resilient and proactive security solutions that safeguard their people, assets and future.

01
Utmost integrity

The expensive version: telling a prospect that a cloud platform would serve them better than ours, and telling a client that the finding they wanted downgraded is staying where it is.

02
Proactive defense

Finding it before someone else does. Everything on the Detect side of our capability map exists because the alternative is learning about your exposure from an attacker.

03
Resilience & adaptability

Assume something will get through eventually, and measure yourself on what happens next. Rehearsal is the cheapest control in security and the least often bought.

Delivery outcomes: Trust  ·  Agility  ·  Resilience
